Ideas & thoughts

QUESTION

How do small systems shape behaviour?

Processes do not just organise work. They also influence what people notice, repeat, and avoid.

IDEA

Make the useful thing easier.

A good system often reduces the number of decisions someone must make to do the right thing.

QUESTION

What makes a story stay?

Is it the character, the timing, the emotion, or the question the story leaves unanswered?

These are prompts for exploration, not settled claims.
