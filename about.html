A PERSONAL INTRODUCTION

Somewhere between numbers and narratives.

An introduction to the person, not just the things he likes.

Hey, I’m Ravie. I live in Dehradun, India, and work in the world of business, people, finance and everyday problem-solving.

depending on the day, i might be overthinker

Someone who can turn one small thought into a full committee meeting in their head.

01 / THE PRACTICAL SIDE

So, what do I actually do?

My professional life involves making sense of things that need structure: processes, responsibilities, numbers, documents and the small details that can make a business run better—or make an otherwise simple task unnecessarily complicated.

I’m pursuing an MBA in Finance (2026–2028), preparing for CMA India Final, and continuing to deepen my understanding of accounting, research, and business. I also hold a SEBI Research Analyst certificate.

I like work that has a purpose. Something that starts as a problem, becomes a plan, and eventually turns into something that actually works. I’m interested not just in getting things done, but in understanding how they could be done better.

I’m practical by necessity, curious by nature, and still figuring out how to become better at both.

02 / THE CURIOUS SIDE

And who am I outside work?

I’m someone who rarely stays interested in just one subject for very long.

A film might lead me to a question about human behaviour. A historical event might make me think about power, ambition or the mistakes people keep repeating. A poem might express something I’ve felt but never quite managed to put into words.

And sometimes, one innocent question turns into an evening of reading, researching and opening seventeen browser tabs that I fully intend to organise later.

I enjoy understanding the connections between things: how people think, why societies change, what stories reveal about us, and why the same human problems seem to return in different forms throughout history.

I’m drawn to books, cinema, philosophy, psychology and the kind of conversations that leave you thinking long after they’ve ended. I don’t claim to be an expert in all these things. I’m simply interested enough to keep learning.

03 / STILL BECOMING

I’m still becoming the person I want to be.

I don’t have a perfectly organised life, a grand five-year plan for every aspect of it, or an answer to every question I ask.

I’m working on my professional growth, continuing my studies, improving how I communicate, and learning to turn ideas into actual projects rather than leaving them as interesting possibilities.

Some days I’m disciplined and focused. Other days, I can spend more time thinking about the best way to do something than actually doing it.

I’m learning that progress isn’t always dramatic. Sometimes it’s getting better at a skill, handling a difficult situation with more patience, admitting that you were wrong, or finally finishing something you’ve been putting off.

I want to become someone who can think clearly, work responsibly, communicate honestly and remain curious without needing to have everything figured out. That feels like a worthwhile direction.

04 / A FEW ANCHORS

A few things I believe.

Not commandments. Just ideas I keep coming back to.

01

Knowledge isn’t the same as wisdom.

You can know a great deal and still have a lot to learn about people, patience and yourself.

02

Take the work seriously.

Do the work responsibly, but don’t become so serious that you forget to laugh at yourself.

03

Curiosity needs humility.

Question your own assumptions, not just everyone else’s. Being willing to learn matters more than appearing right.

04

People are more than first impressions.

A job title tells you something about someone’s work. It tells you very little about their character.

05

Humour should make room, not harm.

A little irony can make difficult conversations easier. It should never come at the expense of someone’s dignity.

06

Not everything needs a grand plan.

You don’t need to turn every interest into a career, every thought into a conclusion, or every day into a personal-growth project.

05 / WHAT YOU’LL FIND HERE

A little work. A little wonder.

Probably someone who enjoys a thoughtful conversation, appreciates a good story, asks a few more questions than necessary, and occasionally finds humour in the most ordinary things.

I’m interested in meeting people who are curious about the world, passionate about something they do, or simply willing to have an honest conversation without pretending to know everything.

You don’t have to share my interests. You don’t even have to agree with my opinions. I’d rather have an interesting disagreement than a conversation in which everyone is trying to sound impressive.

And if you’re having a difficult day, feeling a little lost, or just want to talk to someone without needing a particular reason, you’re welcome to say hello, too. You don’t have to arrive with an interesting topic or the perfect words.

Sometimes, it’s enough to be interested, to pay attention, and to keep going.

ONE LAST THING

This isn’t a finished version of me.

I’m building this website to collect the things I’m learning, the ideas I’m exploring, the stories that stay with me, and the projects I want to make real.

Some of it will be professional. Some of it will be personal. Some of it might change as I change my mind—which, given the overthinking, is not an entirely unlikely possibility.

Think of this website as a record of an ongoing process: a little work, a little reading, a few experiments, some questions, and the occasional unnecessarily long thought.

I’m glad you found your way here.
— ravie
