02 / THE THINGS THAT GUIDE ME

Values & beliefs

Not a manifesto. Just a few principles I keep returning to.

01

Stay curious. Especially when it is inconvenient.

Questions are useful when they challenge my own assumptions, not only everyone else’s. I’d rather keep learning than protect the comfort of being right.

02

Be practical, but leave room for wonder.

Good systems matter. So do stories, art, history, and the strange little ideas that make an ordinary day feel bigger.

03

Take the work seriously. Not yourself all the time.

Responsibility and humour can live in the same room. A little perspective helps when the spreadsheet has become more dramatic than the situation.

04

Be kind without making a performance of it.

People are usually carrying context we cannot see. I want to be thoughtful in how I speak, disagree, and show up.

05

Progress is allowed to look ordinary.

Sometimes growth is a finished project. Sometimes it is asking for help, changing your mind, or trying again without announcing a new era.

06

Build things that are useful.

An idea becomes more interesting when it helps someone, solves a real problem, or makes a complicated thing a little clearer.

These are current working beliefs, not commandments. I reserve the right to learn something and revise the list.
