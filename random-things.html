04 / THE UNNECESSARY DETAILS

Random things about me

The small, slightly odd footnotes that make a person more than a list of interests.

01

I collect questions.

Sometimes the question is more interesting than the answer. Sometimes it is just a very elaborate way to delay doing the dishes.

02

I like connections.

A business process, a line of poetry, and a historical turning point can all be ways of understanding how people behave.

03

I appreciate good explanations.

If something complicated can be explained simply without making it simplistic, you have my attention.

04

I am a collector of open tabs.

Research begins with one thing and ends with a suspicious number of browser tabs and a new topic for later.

05

I enjoy a thoughtful disagreement.

Being challenged respectfully can be more useful than being agreed with automatically.

06

I like stories that stay.

The best films and books do not end when the screen goes dark or the last page turns. They follow you around a bit.
