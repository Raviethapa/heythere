03 / OPEN QUESTIONS

Questions I keep

A list of questions I want to carry around for a while instead of rushing to answer.

PEOPLE

How much of what we call personality is habit?

And how much changes when the setting, incentives, or people around us change?

WORK

Why do simple processes become complicated?

Which complications are necessary, and which are just history nobody has revisited?

HISTORY

Which patterns do societies keep repeating?

What changes when people learn from the past—and what does not?

SELF

When is more thinking useful, and when is it avoidance?

A question for the overthinker’s committee meeting.
