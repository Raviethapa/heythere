06 / NOTES TO SELF

Life lessons (still learning)

Not rules for anyone else. Just lessons I am trying to practise rather than merely admire.

LESSON 01

You can be prepared and still be uncertain.

Confidence does not always arrive before action. Sometimes it is built by doing the thing while still having questions.

LESSON 02

Clarity is often kinder than cleverness.

Whether at work or in a conversation, making something understandable is usually more valuable than making it sound impressive.

LESSON 03

Not every thought deserves a meeting.

Thinking is useful. Rehearsing the same possibility for the ninth time may simply be thinking wearing a fake moustache.

LESSON 04

Consistency beats occasional grand declarations.

Small actions, repeated quietly, tend to change more than a dramatic promise made at midnight.

LESSON 05

People are more complicated than first impressions.

Leave space for context. You rarely know the whole story from one moment.

LESSON 06

Rest is not a reward for becoming a machine.

Work matters, ambition matters, and so does being a person who occasionally stops working.

This list is meant to change as I do. Lessons that never get tested are just attractive sentences.
